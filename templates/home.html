Your week, on the 8 day rotation

Enter your schedule once, or upload the PDF from Veracross, and this lays out your actual week: rotation letters, lunch tracks, Wednesday timings, special schedules and no-school days from the published calendar.
There are two ways to use it. They are the same app; the only difference is where your schedule is kept.
No account
Your schedule is kept in this browser. Nothing is sent to us and nothing is stored on our server, so there is nothing of yours for us to lose or look at. No email, no verification code, no password.

The cost: it lives in one browser on one device, and clearing your browsing data deletes it. On an iPhone you need to add it to your Home Screen, which the app will walk you through.
Use it without an account
With an account
Your schedule is on every device you log in on, and it survives a new phone or a cleared browser. Version history keeps your earlier versions in case you paste over something.

The cost: the text is stored on our server, where an administrator could in principle reach it. Sign up needs a @shadysideacademy.org address and a code sent to it.
Log in or sign up
You are not stuck with the one you pick. Both read and write the same file, so you can export from either and import into the other whenever you want.
