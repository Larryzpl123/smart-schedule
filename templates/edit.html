Line 1 = your name
Line 2 = grade, e.g. 27 - Junior

Each semester starts with:
@term Fall 2025 = 08.26-01.16
(label = MM.DD-MM.DD date range)

Class lines (period A-H):
A - Name - Num - Sec - Teacher - Room - color
colors: red green blue yellow purple teal orange pink gray indigo cyan
Shady Side colours by department, and a PDF import uses those: Math red, Science green, English blue, History yellow, World Language purple, Computer Science orange, Art cyan, Music teal.

Two optional extras on a class line, any order:
- La or - Ea: which lunch track. Only changes the midday class; your PDF says which.
- days 2,6: only meets on those rotation days. Use it for a lab that borrows an otherwise-free period.
A - Physics 2 - PY430 - 1 - Ms. Rivera - MC 210 - blue - La - days 2,6

Your own events (max {{ caps.events }}), any of:
@event 2026-09-10 - Meet Mr. B - 11:50-12:40 - gray
@event Mon,Wed - Piano - 16:00-17:00 - 09.01-12.20 - pink
@event monthly Thu#1 - Form Meeting - 10:05-10:30 - pink (Thu#1 = first Thursday, Thu#-1 = last, monthly 15 = the 15th)
@event every 90 from 2026-09-07 - Renew hosting - 19:00-19:15 - gray — a fixed number of days from a date, for the things that fall on no weekday and no day of the month.

Homework (max {{ caps.hw }}) hangs on a class letter, not a time:
@hw A - before - Read ch.4 - 2026-09-10 (before or during)
Two optional fields on the end: which project it belongs to, and done:
@hw E - before - Prospectus - 2026-09-25 - US History Paper - done

Projects (max 12) are long pieces of work. They have no time of day, so they are drawn as a band across the days they cover rather than as a block:
@project US History Paper - 2026-09-14 - 2027-01-15 - blue
Progress is counted from the homework attached to it, never typed in.

Optional per term:
@immersive Your Move - IM327 - Mr. Lindqvist - MC 104 - indigo

Sports (max 8) are not part of a term: three seasons, two semesters, and winter crosses January, so each one carries its own dates and they go above the first @term:
@sport Swimming - Mon,Thu - 15:45-17:15 - 11.16-02.20 - cyan
The MM.DD-MM.DD season is optional; leave it out and it runs all year.

Preferences (once, at the end):
@pref time_format = 12h

Rotation, community time, finals and the Wednesday schedule are shared by the whole school, you don't add those.
